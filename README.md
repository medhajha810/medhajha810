engineering**. I'm currently an **Engineering Apprentice at Infineon Technologies**, and previously a
**Software Engineer Intern & Founding Contributor at TeachGenie.ai**, a DPIIT-recognised EdTech startup.

in applying AI to **healthcare, education and sustainability**.
